Gallery surface textures

Stone: Marble 01, Poly Haven
https://polyhaven.com/a/marble_01
Source area: 1.5 m × 1.5 m.

Wood: Wood Table, Poly Haven
https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_table
Source area: 1 m × 1 m.

Both texture sets are distributed by Poly Haven under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal.
https://polyhaven.com/license
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

These WebP derivatives were made from the original diffuse, OpenGL normal, and roughness maps. Diffuse maps are 1024 × 1024 sRGB. Normal and roughness maps are 512 × 512 data textures.

White painted plaster: White Plaster 02 by Rob Tuytel, Poly Haven.
https://polyhaven.com/a/white_plaster_02
Source area: 1 m × 1 m. Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal.
The diffuse scan is desaturated and lifted to a neutral gallery-white base.
The 512 × 512 normal map retains the source OpenGL convention.

Exterior landscape panorama: Shady Patch by Greg Zaal, Poly Haven.
https://polyhaven.com/a/shady_patch
Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal. The woodland.webp derivative is a
2048 × 1024 equirectangular sRGB image from the original HDR panorama;
exposure and foliage saturation have been adjusted for the gallery windows.
