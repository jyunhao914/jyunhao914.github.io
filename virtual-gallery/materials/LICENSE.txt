Gallery surface textures

Stone: Marble 01, Poly Haven
https://polyhaven.com/a/marble_01
Source area: 1.5 m × 1.5 m.

Wood: Wood Table, Poly Haven
https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_table
Source area: 1 m × 1 m.

Both texture sets are distributed by Poly Haven under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal.
https://polyhaven.com/license
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

These WebP derivatives were made from the original diffuse, OpenGL normal, and roughness maps. Diffuse maps are 1024 × 1024 sRGB. Normal and roughness maps are 512 × 512 data textures.

White painted plaster: White Plaster 02 by Rob Tuytel, Poly Haven.
https://polyhaven.com/a/white_plaster_02
Source area: 1 m × 1 m. Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal.
The diffuse scan is desaturated and lifted to a neutral gallery-white base.
The 512 × 512 normal map retains the source OpenGL convention.

Exterior landscape panorama: Shady Patch by Greg Zaal, Poly Haven.
https://polyhaven.com/a/shady_patch
Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal. The woodland.webp derivative is a
2048 × 1024 equirectangular sRGB image from the original HDR panorama;
exposure and foliage saturation have been adjusted for the gallery windows.
canopy-light.webp is a softened luminance mask of the same photographed canopy
used to project leaf shadows onto the garden approach.

Wood Floor (Dimitrios Savva), 1.70 m scan. https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_floor
Quatrefoil Jacquard Fabric (colormass and Rico Cilliers), 0.28 m scan; diffuse recolored for scroll mounting. https://polyhaven.com/a/quatrefoil_jacquard_fabric
Both assets are CC0.

Mount materials in mount/: the walnut, oak, ash and teak finish simulations use
different colour-neutralized, seamless board sections from the CC0 Wood Table
and Wood Floor scans credited above. Their names describe the simulated finish.
The brushed surface, cotton board, plain silk and linen maps are procedurally
generated for this gallery.

The floral-twill (花綾) colour, OpenGL normal and roughness maps are original
procedural fabric textures. The plum-blossom design uses a single dye with
opposing diagonal twill directions across the flowers and ground.

Limestone for the entrance and stone reveals: Medieval Blocks 05, Rob Tuytel,
Poly Haven (formerly Texture Haven), Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal.
https://polyhaven.com/a/medieval_blocks_05
https://polyhaven.com/license
Source area 2 m × 2 m. Only exposed limestone faces were sampled from the
matching diffuse and OpenGL normal maps, excluding mortar and soil. The
seamless derivatives have a 0.70 m × 0.52 m repeat. The albedo is colour
balanced for a light honed finish and the averaged normals are renormalized.
limestone-color.webp is sRGB and limestone-normal.webp is non-colour data.

Garden surroundings and glazing reflections: Sunny Vondelpark by Greg Zaal, Poly Haven.
https://polyhaven.com/a/sunny_vondelpark
CC0 1.0 Universal. garden-canopy.webp is a tone-mapped, colour-graded and oriented
2816 × 1408 derivative of the 4K HDR panorama. The landscape foreground, pavilion
and courtyard are site geometry.

Previous garden panorama: Ballawley Park by Savva Zakharov, Poly Haven.
https://polyhaven.com/a/ballawley_park
CC0 1.0 Universal. garden-panorama.webp is retained for cached versions.

Garden lawn: Grass Ground by Charlotte Baglioni, Poly Haven.
https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_ground
CC0 1.0 Universal. garden-grass.webp is a 768 px diffuse-map derivative.

Polished reception floor: Granite Tile by Charlotte Baglioni, Poly Haven.
https://polyhaven.com/a/granite_tile
CC0 1.0 Universal. reception-granite-color.webp is a black-graded derivative of
the diffuse scan. reception-granite-normal.webp uses the OpenGL normal map and
reception-granite-rough.webp is graded for the polished indoor finish.

Reception counter: Marble Rock 02 by Amal Kumar, Poly Haven.
https://polyhaven.com/a/marble_rock_02
CC0 1.0 Universal. reception-counter-stone.webp uses the diffuse scan, with a
warm-grey colour grade for the cut and polished counter slabs.

pier-daylight.webp is a reduced derivative of garden-canopy.webp for the metal lettering reflections, from the Sunny Vondelpark panorama credited above.

Directory plaques: Granite Tile by Charlotte Baglioni, Poly Haven (CC0).
https://polyhaven.com/a/granite_tile
directory-granite-{color,normal,rough}.webp contain six separate interior cuts
from the matching 4K diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness scans, excluding grout.

atrium-pure-sky.webp is a daylight colour grade of Kloofendal 38d Partly
Cloudy (Pure Sky), by Greg Zaal and Jarod Guest, Poly Haven (CC0).
https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_38d_partly_cloudy_puresky

atrium-interior-reflection.webp is a render of the public reception hall from
the skylight curb, using the architectural surfaces credited above.

Calligraphy study windows: study-garden-detail.webp is a cropped higher-resolution
colour-matched derivative of the Shady Patch photograph by Greg Zaal (CC0), as
credited above. It registers to woodland.webp in the visitors' sightline.
